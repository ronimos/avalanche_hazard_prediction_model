Reading the numbers

Each value is the probability of at least one D2 or larger dry slab avalanche in the polygon that day, natural or triggered, whether or not anyone reports it, at the activity and observation level of the most closely watched polygons. Wet avalanches are not included.

The model assumes every polygon produces avalanches at the same rate under the same snowpack. That holds best for natural avalanches; triggered ones also depend on traffic, which isn't measured. A quiet polygon's own number of avalanches may be lower than its value here. Rank days within a polygon with confidence; compare polygons with care.

The 80% range covers the model's statistical uncertainty, not that assumption.__SEASON_NOTE__
